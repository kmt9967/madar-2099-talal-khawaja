The land

One plot. One horizon. Nothing in the way.

The site is a level basin ringed by dunes, with firm ground for foundations and open sky in every direction. The whole plan fits inside one circle you could walk across in forty minutes.

The masterplan

Built from the centre out.

Construction starts at the Spire and moves outward ring by ring. Each ring is finished and lived in before the next one begins, so the city is complete at every stage of its growth.

Eight avenues run from the centre to the boundary. Everything between them is walkable, shaded and free of traffic.
